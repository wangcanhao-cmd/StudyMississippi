Why study in MS?

The Hospitality State as a study destination.

The 2018 StudyMississippi profile highlighted a temperate climate, friendly communities, scenic beaches, musical heritage, cuisine, varied academic fields, and comparatively affordable study options. It also described Mississippi institutions in rural, suburban, and urban settings.

Academic rangeAgriculture, aerospace, engineering, management, international business, law, pharmacy, arts and sciences, veterinary and medical sciences, nursing, and more.
Culture & placeHospitality, music, food, beaches, and communities across the state.
Education & careersCollaboration between companies and higher education institutions as part of Mississippi's education and career landscape.

Historic descriptive content is based on the StudyMississippi profile in the 2018 NAFSA brochure and is presented as legacy material rather than current statistical claims.
